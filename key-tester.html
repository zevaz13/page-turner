    Many remotes sleep to save the battery, and the first press after a pause can be slow or lost. Choose an idle time, keep your hands off the remote, and press it as soon as the screen turns teal. Run each length a few times: differences under about 100 ms are just human reaction noise. The 5 s run is your baseline.
